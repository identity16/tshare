Waitlist privacy

Updated November 3, 2026. This notice covers the tshare Cloud waitlist, operated by the developer behind identity16/tshare. Cloud has not launched.

What we collect

Your email, plan interest, optional use case, signup source, and the time and version of your consent. We store this in a private database. Do not include confidential files or personal information about other people in your use case.

Why we collect it

To understand demand and contact you about Cloud launch or early access, with your consent. Signing up does not create an account, reserve a price, or charge you. The form does not send an email automatically. We do not sell the list or publish it.

Hosting and access

This site uses ChatGPT Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure to serve pages and store submissions. Providers may process network metadata to deliver and protect the service. Short-lived address hashes are used to limit repeated submissions; raw addresses are not stored in our waitlist database. The operator can export entries through an owner-only endpoint.

Your removal link

After signup, save the private removal link shown on screen. Anyone holding that link can remove that submission. Repeated signups do not replace the original link. Removal deletes the active waitlist record; provider backups may persist under their retention policies.

Retention

We will review unused entries within 12 months and remove information no longer needed for a launch decision. You can remove your submission earlier using your private link. This notice will be revisited before Cloud launches.

Self-hosted stays separate

Your self-hosted artifacts are not uploaded to this website or included in the waitlist. The app does not send us your library data or usage telemetry.
